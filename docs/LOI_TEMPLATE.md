# MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING / LETTER OF INTENT (LOI)

**BETWEEN:**
1. **Lyt Energy Technologies Ltd.** ("Technology Partner")

**DATE:** [Date]  
**SUBJECT:** Pilot Deployment of Lyt AI Mini-Grid Climate-Resilience & Edge Dispatch Platform

---

### 1. Purpose & Scope
This Letter of Intent records the mutual intent of the Parties to conduct a field validation pilot of the **Lyt Platform** across [Number, e.g. 3 to 5] operational mini-grid sites in [Region / District, e.g. Northern Province, Sierra Leone / Kédougou, Senegal].

The objectives of the Pilot are:
1. Eliminate unnecessary diesel backup generator run-time through probabilistic solar recharge forecasting.
2. Mathematically enforce an uninterrupted 3-hour minimum electrical reserve for rural health clinics and vaccine cold-chains.
3. Detect Harmattan dust accumulation and inverter degradation using edge-based physical residual analysis.
4. Broadcast tomorrow's solar availability to local community members via low-cost USSD and SMS in native languages.

---

### 2. Responsibilities of Technology Partner (Lyt)
- Provide pre-configured Raspberry Pi Edge Gateways equipped with the offline-first Rust Edge Agent.
- Grant secure access to the Regional Atlas, Operator Console, and Verified Impact Ledger.
- Configure site-specific digital twins, inverter telemetry mappings, and local language SMS/USSD templates.
- Deliver technical training and 24/7 technical support to Project Partner field technicians.

---

### 3. Responsibilities of Project Partner (Mini-Grid Operator)
- Grant physical and network access for gateway installation at designated mini-grid inverter substations.
- Provide historical generation and consumption meter data for baseline calibration.
- Designate primary technician points of contact to participate in operational alert acknowledgment workflows.
- Facilitate community engagement and user feedback collection on SMS/USSD village broadcast alerts.

---

### 4. Pilot Timeline & Key Milestones
- **Weeks 1–2**: Hardware gateway deployment and local Modbus/MQTT telemetry verification.
- **Weeks 3–6**: Baseline passive monitoring and neural model calibration against local microclimates.
- **Weeks 7–12**: Full automated MPC dispatch with critical healthcare reserve locking and community USSD broadcasting.
- **Week 13**: Final Impact & Reliability Audit Report presentation based on exportable Ledger data.

---

### 5. Non-Binding Nature & Confidentiality
This Letter of Intent reflects the sincere intentions of the Parties. Except for confidentiality obligations regarding site telemetry and proprietary algorithms, this document is non-binding and does not constitute a financial commitment until formal execution of the definitive Pilot Agreement.
